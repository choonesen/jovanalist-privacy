LoopLockers Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 August 2026

LoopLockers does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information. Everything you type into it stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDK of any kind in the app. LoopLockers contains no networking code, so there is no server for your data to be sent to — not ours, and not anyone else's.

What the app stores, and where

Your promises, shopping list, urgent items, streak history, settings and counters are saved on your device only, in the app's own private storage. That data is covered by your device's protections and by your own encrypted iCloud or iTunes backups, if you make them.

Backups you make yourself

Settings ▸ Your data ▸ Save a backup writes everything the app knows into a single JSON file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice — Files, AirDrop, a message, iCloud Drive — and it leaves the app only because you asked it to. LoopLockers does not upload it, does not keep a copy, and has nowhere to send one.

The file is plain text and is not encrypted, so treat it the way you would treat a note containing the same things. Restore from a backup reads a file you pick and replaces what is on the device with it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, LoopLockers schedules local reminders — the ones your iPhone creates and delivers by itself. Nothing about your tasks is sent anywhere in order to produce them, and no push server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in Settings inside the app, or in iOS Settings ▸ Notifications.

A reminder you set on a particular item shows that item's own words, so its text is handed to iOS to be displayed and will appear on your lock screen. It goes no further than your device. If you would rather it did not appear there, iOS Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ LoopLockers ▸ Show Previews controls that for every app.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings ▸ Your data ▸ Start over erases every promise, list, streak and counter immediately.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
  • Any backup file you exported is yours, wherever you put it. Deleting the app does not delete those, because the app never knew where they went.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to send us.

Children

LoopLockers does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects or transmits anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
